The marble temple

April, 2025

Inspiration

Benchamabophit Dusitwanaram Temple, also known as The Marble Temple, is one of the most famous temples in Bangkok, Thailand. Due to the use of traditional Thai symmetrical architecture together with marble imported Italy, it represents a great combination of local culture and the influence of European design.

Process

HScript and VEX played a key role in building the model procedurally in Houdini, enabling a flexible and non-destructive workflow. Custom parameters were set up to easily adjust and fine-tune every aspect of the model throughout the production process.
